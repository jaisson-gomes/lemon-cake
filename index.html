Easy Lemon Loaf Cake

Indulge in a slice of pure sunshine. This moist and zesty lemon cake features a bright, tender crumb topped with a sweet, tangy glaze that melts in your mouth. Delightfully refreshing, it’s the perfect treat to elevate your afternoon tea or coffee break.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp lemon zest
  • ½ cup unsalted butter (melted)
  • 2 large eggs
  • ⅓ cup fresh lemon juice
  • ½ cup whole milk

For the Glaze

  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Preheat and Prepare: Heat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan with butter and flour.
  2. Mix Dry Ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Mix Wet Ingredients: In a large bowl, rub the lemon zest into the sugar with your fingers to release the oils. Whisk in the melted butter, eggs, and lemon juice until smooth.
  4. Combine: Alternately add the dry ingredients and the milk to the wet mixture. Stir gently just until combined. Do not overmix.
  5. Bake: Pour the batter into the prepared pan. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Cool: Let the cake cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer it to a wire rack to cool completely.
  7. Glaze: Whisk the powdered sugar and lemon juice together until smooth. Pour over the cooled cake before slicing.

Nutrition Information

Per Slice (1/12 of the cake):

  • Calories: 280 kcal
  • Fat: 12g
  • Carbohydrates: 38g
  • Protein: 4g

Baker's Tips

  • Perfect Drizzle: For a beautiful glaze, drizzle the powdered sugar and lemon juice mixture over the cooled cake in a slow, circular motion.
  • Room Temperature:Make sure your eggs and milk are at room temperature to avoid a curdled batter.
  • Storage: Store the cake in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.

Variations

  • Lemon Poppy Seed: Add 2 tablespoons of poppy seeds to the batter for a delightful crunch.
  • Lemon Blueberry: Fold in 1 cup of fresh or frozen blueberries for a fruity twist.
  • Lemon Almond: Add 1 teaspoon of almond extract to the batter for a nutty flavor.

Serving Suggestions

  • Serve with a dollop of whipped cream and a sprinkle of lemon zest for an elegant presentation.
  • Pair with a cup of Earl Grey tea or freshly brewed coffee for a delightful afternoon treat.
  • Top with fresh berries for added color and flavor.

Storage Instructions

  • Room Temperature: Store the cake in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.
  • Refrigeration: You can refrigerate the cake for up to 5 days, but let it reach room temperature before serving.
  • Freezing:Wrap individual slices tightly in plastic wrap and freeze for up to 2 months.

Conclusion

This easy lemon loaf cake is a perfect balance of sweet and tangy flavors, making it a delightful treat for any occasion. Enjoy baking and savoring every bite!
